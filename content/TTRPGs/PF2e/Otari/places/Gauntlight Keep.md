### Notes on the Gauntlight Keep
_As compiled by the adventurers during their descent into the ruins and from the words of [[Wrin Sivinxi]]_
#### The Gauntlight: An Overview
Once only a looming silhouette in the fog north of [[Otari]], Gauntlight Keep has revealed itself to be far more than a ruin. Rising like a cyclopean finger from the bog, the lighthouse-like tower contains within and beneath it a vast network of dungeons, secrets, and dark history.
#### Historical Knowledge (as told by [[Wrin Sivinxi]])
- [[Belcorra]] was a powerful sorcerer, possibly necromancer, who made her lair within Gauntlight long ago. Her magic was focused on undeath and dominion, and her ultimate ambitions remain unclear.
- [[Belcorra]] terrorised the surrounding region, raising the dead and summoning horrors from unknown places.
- The town of [[Otari]] was [[The Founding of Otari|founded]] in the aftermath of this threat. Its namesake, [[Otari Ilvashti]], led a party of adventurers to confront [[Belcorra]]. He died in the assault, but his allies defeated her, later building the town in his honour.

---

#### **Exploration Summary**
- **Surface Ruins**:
    - The crumbling remains of the old keep.
    - Within these ruins, the party discovered the Mudspitter Goblins, a clan of opportunistic scavengers and breeders of oversized maggots.
    - Machival negotiated a tentative agreement with the [[Goblin|goblins]], leveraging their survival instincts for his own aims.
- **First Subterranean Level — The Servants’ Quarters**:
    - A maze of dust-choked halls, collapsed rooms, and ancient servant corridors.
    - The adventurers encountered a tribe of [[Morlock|Morlocks]], strange subterranean humanoids who seem to have taken up residence. Their culture is violent but survivable, and a temporary peace was established.
    - They found signs of old necromantic activity, including an offering chamber bearing signs of sacrifice—possibly connected to [[Cult of the Ghost Queen|Belcorra’s cult]].

- **Second Subterranean Level — The Library**:
    - Here they met the [[Cult of the Ghost Queen]], undead [[Ghoul|ghouls]] who revere [[Belcorra]] as a divine figure.
    - The cult, maintains the ancient knowledge within the library. Some members are intelligent and even cooperative—so long as the party does not threaten their faith.
    - The party discovered a sealed passage leading to a [[Gibbering Mouther]], a horrid manifestation of flesh and madness, which attempted to consume one of the [[Cult of the Ghost Queen|cult’s]] members.

---

#### **Current Conclusions**

- **The Gauntlight is waking.**  
    The keep’s long-dormant magics seem to be stirring—especially after the undead recently rose in [[Otari Graveyard|Otari’s graveyard]], likely triggered by the Gauntlight’s power.
- **The Cult is active.**  
    [[Belcorra|The Ghost Queen’s]] worshippers below are not passive—they maintain the old structures, defend [[Belcorra|Belcorra’s]] legacy, and may be part of a larger, slower-moving plan.
- **The Deeper Levels Await.**  
    [[The Party|The party]] has only scratched the surface of the keep. [[Belcorra]] may be long dead… but the powers she commanded still churn deep below.
