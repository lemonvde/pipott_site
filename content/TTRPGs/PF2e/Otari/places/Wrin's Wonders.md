_60 Osprey St._

An eccentric little shop of curiosities and magical oddities located in the town of [[Otari]]. Run by the enigmatic [[Wrin Sivinxi]], the shop is filled with exotic trinkets, strange artefacts, astrological charts, and other mysterious items from across Golarion and beyond. With its beaded curtains, swirling incense, and shelves stacked high with unusual relics, Wrin's Wonders feels more like a fortune-teller’s tent than a traditional store.

# Interactions with the Players
- This is where [[Glock's Harrowing]] took place.
- Saw [[Wrin Sivinxi|Wrin]], [[Glock]], and [[Machival]] defend themselves against an ambush from [[Yinyasmera]] and her goons.
- Has served as a pseudo safe-house for [[The Party|the party]] on multiple occasions.
