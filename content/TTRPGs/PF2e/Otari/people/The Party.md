The brave heroes that find themselves in the middle of Otari's problems are:
- [[Caligo]]
- [[Glock]]
- [[Jhaan]]
- [[Machival]]
- [[Radagast]]
All in the sea-side town of [[Otari]] for their own personal reasons, the group did not get along or fall into trust easily.

Their [[The Story So Far|story so far]] has seen them uncover a secret [[Kobold]] nest underneath the town, where a young [[Horned Dragon]] was being fed the [[Otari Fishery|fishery's]] stock. With clever thinking, and a smattering of betrayal, the heroes wiped out the [[Kobold|kobolds]] and escaped the [[Horned Dragon|dragon's]] jaws to warn the town of the present danger.

Without any rest, and even more betrayal, the next crisis revealed itself in the form of a bright, and powerful beam of energy landing in the [[Otari Graveyard|graveyard]] and raising the corpses of those resting there.

Now the brave adventurers are working to uncover the root cause for such dangerous, and careless magics.
