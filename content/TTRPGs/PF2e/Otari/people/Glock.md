</div></div><h1>Beginnings</h1>

Glock was Orphaned on the streets of [[Otari]]. Taken in by [[Yinyasmera]], tavern owner of [[Crook's Nook]]. Not out of love, but out of opportunity. Forced to fight for everything; food, shelter, a place in the family. Glock's fighting skills were forged through pain and hardship, resulting in a deep-seated survival instinct, and a mistrust of others.

Glock's distant [[Orc]] heritage began to show through puberty, as he quickly grew in size and began to display inhuman strength. Glock earned renown in [[Otari]] by fighting in secret underground fighting rings, held in secret locations underneath [[Otari]]. [[Yinyasmera]] began to rig fights to her favour, and Glock played his part.

<h1>Absalom</h1>

[[Yinyasmera]] maximised her earning potential by taking Glock, now on the cusp of adulthood, to the nearby city of [[Absalom]] - centre of the world. Glock was entered into bigger and deadlier fights at high society gatherings, experiencing the allure of burgeoning fame and earning a reputation as the "Beast of Otari". His success saw no fortune in his name, but gave [[Yinyasmera]] a foothold in [[Absalom]] to begin expanding her criminal influence.

Tragedy would befall Glock during the [[Tournament of the Seven Sisters]]. Seven daughters of a prominent [[Absalom]] politician, all to be wed within the year, would host an extravagant tournament throughout their wedding festivities. The seventh due to marry, [[Miveyn]], took a fancy to the intimidating young Glock over the course of the tournament, and they entered a secret courtship. Her betrothed, shipping mogul [[Shochem Avadere]], paid her no real attention until gossip reached him of their betrayal. [[Miveyn]] was found strangled during the wedding of the sixth sister, with blame quickly pinned on the 'murderous' Beast of Otari.

Glock barely escaped with his life, although the overwhelming cocktail of guilt, despair and anger would manifest in his first true Rage. A massacre of guards and well-intentioned guests would be the only true crime that Glock was responsible for before fleeing, ironically sneaking aboard an [[Avadere]] trading vessel bound for a destination unknown.
**Thoughts of [[Miveyn]] and the tournament would subsequently be a source of barbaric rage.

# Almas
Glock's banishment from [[Absalom]] would also see [[Yinyasmera]] blacklisted from [[Absalom]] high-society and her criminal network quickly scattered. Believing the worst of him, [[Yinyasmera]] would dedicate a considerable portion of her time and fortune to hunting down Glock. If Glock were to dare set foot in [[Absalom]] again, he would also be arrested on sight.

Aboard the [[Avadere]] trading vessel, Glock hid and stole food to sustain himself for a time before being discovered. Luckily his discovery was by an old [[Half-Orc]] by the name of [[Vorrix]], who would take pity on him and smuggle him off the ship at the next port of [[Almas]].
Wise in his years, but also frail, [[Vorrix]] took the young Glock on as protection for his personal quest - Glock was never told the full truth of [[Vorrix|Vorrix's]] purpose, all he understood was that [[Vorrix]] searched for an artefact tied to an ancient god, and traded his wares for rumours and secrets in pursuit of this. 

Keeping a low profile in [[Almas]] was initially difficult to the disgruntled Glock, and to assuage his dangerous fits of rage, [[Vorrix]] would teach Glock much about [[Orc]] tradition and mysticism, teaching Glock a level of inner peace through battle mantras and spiritual worship. Glock would here learn the [[Orc]] practice of painting a War Mask to attune his focus and concentration. 

For a couple of years, Glock and [[Vorrix]] built trust in one another as they ventured in and out of [[Almas]] on their quest, even fending off a couple contracted assassination attempts courtesy of the vengeful [[Yinyasmera]]. However, this would ultimately lead to [[Vorrix|Vorrix's]] demise, when Glock would one day return from gathering supplies to find his mentor fatally wounded.
Jumped by the waiting assassins, Glock flew into a blind rage and was able to kill all but one, choosing to aid [[Vorrix]] instead of chase the fleeing killer with a bleeding dagger tattoo.
In a familiar panic of guilt and fear, and in the throes of rage, Glock attempted to heal his mentor with some of the spiritual magic he had witnessed [[Vorrix]] perform.

Inadvertently, whilst Glock's actions would not be successful in saving his mentor, they would prevent [[Vorrix]] from fully departing this world, causing an aspect of his mentor's soul to attach to Glock in his emotional state. From this point on, Glock's rage would invoke possession by a part of [[Vorrix|Vorrix's]] spirit - a spiritually attuned rage known as Spirit Instinct.
# Otari
Abandoning his mentor's quest, Glock sought revenge against [[Yinyasmera]] back in [[Otari]]. Arriving in [[Otari]] for his big showdown, Glock would learn that [[Yinyasmera]] no longer resided in [[Crook's Nook]], and had vanished from [[Otari]] completely. Only whispers of a spy network that remained in the shadows beneath [[Otari]] left an impression of [[Yinyasmera|Yinyasmera's]] legacy.

Glock, defeated for purpose and back in a place he once called home, saw the potential to start over anew in [[Otari]] by using his strength and skills to aid townsfolk and traveller's alike on their various quests - whether an honest attempt to reclaim some of that adventurous spirit his time under [[Vorrix]] had gifted him, or just a means to buy time until he could find [[Yinyasmera]] - not even Glock truly knows.
