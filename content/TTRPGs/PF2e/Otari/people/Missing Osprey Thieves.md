The [[Osprey Club]] — an elusive network of rogues, smugglers, and information brokers under the control of [[Yinyasmera]], former proprietor of the Crook’s Nook — recently dispatched a small crew into the depths of [[Gauntlight Keep]]. Their objective, while unclear to most, appeared to centre on locating a teleportation or transportation device — something [[Yinyasmera]] has knowledge of and access to a ritual for.

The party discovered that four thieves had entered the ruins. One perished, sacrificed by cultists. The remaining three were found imprisoned and brutalised by [[Morlock|Morlocks]] in the Servants’ Quarters, under the watch of the brutal [[Morlock]] leader [[Graulgust]] and his cadre of “[[Morlock Engineer|engineers]].”

---

#### The Rescue
- The party encountered the three surviving Osprey Thieves starved, bloodied, and armed only with makeshift shivs, trapped inside a stone prison under constant surveillance.
- Rather than negotiate with [[Graulgust]], [[The Party|the adventurers]] hatched a plan involving illusion, trickery, and ambush. Through a combination of distraction ([[Caligo|Caligo’s]] illusion of [[Augrael]]), performance ([[Glock|Glock’s]] staged brawl), and clever placement, they lured [[Graulgust]] and his guards into the trap.
- A short, brutal fight ensued, in which [[The Party|the party]] and the freed thieves overwhelmed the [[Morlock|Morlocks]]. [[Radagast]], transformed into an ape, crushed enemies with terrifying strength.
- The Osprey Thieves proved useful in the fray, and were temporarily allowed to travel with the party—albeit under thinly veiled threats and intimidation from [[Glock]] and [[Machival]].
- The prisoners shared what they knew: [[Yinyasmera]] had sent them to locate an ancient transportation device hidden somewhere near [[Gauntlight Keep|Gauntlight]], though they were unaware of its exact function or location.
- They followed the adventurers deeper into the ruins, eventually arriving at the lair of the Cult of the Ghost Queen — a faction of intelligent, flesh-craving [[Ghoul|ghouls]].

---

#### **The Sacrifice**
- Upon arrival, the [[Ghoul|ghouls]] demanded a “donation of flesh.”
- After some deliberation the adventurers offered up the three Osprey Thieves as tribute, and for safe passage.
- Despite the thieves’ pleas for help and betrayal-laced screams, the [[Ghoul|ghouls]] eagerly accepted the offering, dragging them into the depths of the library, presumably for consumption or ritual.
- The act was calculated, brutal, and pragmatic

---

#### **Current Status:**
Presumed dead or undead.  
Their ultimate fate remains uncertain, but considering the appetites and practices of the [[Cult of the Ghost Queen|Cult]], it is likely the Ospreys were either devoured, transformed, or reanimated in service to the [[Belcorra|Ghost Queen]].
