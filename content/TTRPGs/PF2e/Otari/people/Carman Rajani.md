<img src="carman-rajani.webp" style="float: right; width: 400px; height: auto; margin-left: 1rem;" />A grouchy, bitter, no-nonsense man, who runs [[Blades for Glades]] in [[Otari]].
Known to [[Jhaan]], and by extension the town guard, Carman briefly trained under [[Yinyasmera]] along-side [[Glock]] at the [[Crook's Nook]]. He was given the  moniker "Ace of Blades" as the best knife thrower in town, a title which he held for so long that it's become his nickname among fellow members of the [[Osprey Club]].

# Interactions with the Party
- Traded briefly with the group before being interrupted by [[Two Businessmen]].
- Accepted a commission to shape the tooth of a [[River Drake]] into a dagger for [[Machival]].
