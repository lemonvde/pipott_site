<img src="wrin-sivinxi.webp" style="float: left; width: 400px; height: auto; margin-right: 1rem;" />With her long, flowing robes stitched in constellations and bracelets that jingle like whispered omens, she carries herself like someone who’s walked more dreams than roads.

She keeps mostly to her shop, [[Wrin's Wonders]], preferring open air and wide spaces. Everyone knows she **fears enclosed places** — not in a quirky, “don’t-like-basements” sort of way, but in a visceral, soul-deep manner. She never goes near the town’s tunnels or cellars. Even her shop’s back room is curtained off, untouched.

# Interactions with the Players
- Performed [[Glock's Harrowing]].
- Defended [[Glock]] and [[Machival]] against [[Yinyasmera]] and her goons.
- Has aided, and healed [[The Party|the party]] on multiple occasions.
